Not just a craft class

More Than a Coconut Shell Workshop

This experience is designed as more than a craft class. During your visit to the ZeroPlastic Impact Center, you will create your own coconut shell lamp while also discovering how natural materials, local craftsmanship and creative reuse can reduce our dependence on single-use plastic.

Make Your Own Lamp

Work with a prepared coconut shell and complete the final sanding, polishing and decorative design yourself.

Experience ZeroPlastic

Meet Mr. Hero, learn about Sri Lanka's plastic challenge and watch discarded plastic bottle lids transformed into a useful souvenir.

Discover Local Craft

Explore a collection of sustainable products sourced from local artisan and craftsman families across Sri Lanka.

Every stage, ready to photograph

From Coconut Shell to Handmade Lamp

We prepare the time-consuming parts before you arrive so you can spend your visit enjoying the most satisfying stages of the craft.

1

Start With a Coconut Shell

Discover how an everyday coconut shell can become a beautiful and useful piece of Sri Lankan craft.

2

Finish the Sanding

Our team completes most of the heavy preparation beforehand. You finish the remaining sanding yourself using progressively finer sandpaper and feel the natural surface become smooth.

3

Polish the Shell

Polish and buff the coconut shell and watch its natural colour and texture transform into a smooth, beautiful finish.

4

Create Your Design

Choose or create a simple pattern and make the small openings that allow light to shine through your lamp.

5

Bring It to Life

See your design come alive as light passes through the pattern you created.

6

Take It Home

Your finished coconut shell lamp becomes a handmade memory of your visit to Sri Lanka.

Small craft tools are used under guidance from our team.

The experience is recommended for guests aged 10 and above. Younger participants should not use craft tools unless specifically approved and directly supervised by our team.

The Workshop in Pictures

A Sri Lankan story

Discover Sri Lanka's Tree of Life

The coconut tree has supported Sri Lankan life for generations, providing food, drink, fibre, timber, household items and craft materials. In this experience, a coconut shell that might otherwise be discarded becomes something beautiful that you create with your own hands.

Take a moment

Pause for a Cup of Herbal Tea

After crafting, enjoy complimentary Sri Lankan herbal tea served in the relaxed setting of the Impact Center. It is a chance to slow down, explore the space and talk with our team about Sri Lankan craft and sustainable living.

Meet the elephant

Meet Mr. Hero

Your experience also includes a visit to Mr. Hero, our elephant sculpture built from salvaged plastic and created to start conversations about plastic pollution. Learn what happens to the plastic we use, why reducing single-use plastic matters and how ZeroPlastic is working with communities to create practical alternatives.

Waste, reimagined

Watch Waste Become Something New

See discarded plastic bottle lids transformed in front of you into a small reusable or decorative item. The demonstration shows how waste can be reimagined while opening a wider conversation about why recycling alone cannot solve the plastic problem.

249 artisan families

Discover Products Made by Local Artisans

Explore more than 1,000 product designs sourced through our network of approximately 249 artisan and craftsman families. Discover practical plastic alternatives, traditional crafts, natural-material products and creative items made using recovered materials.

Buying a product is optional. Visitors are welcome to simply explore the collection and learn about the people and materials behind it.

Before you come

Questions We Get Asked

Is the coconut shell lamp workshop suitable for children?

The experience is recommended for children aged 10 and above. Small craft tools are used during part of the workshop, so children participate under guidance.

How long does the experience take?

Allow approximately one hour for the core workshop, with additional time if you would like to explore the Impact Center and artisan collection.

Do I take my coconut shell lamp home?

Yes. The lamp you finish during the workshop is yours to take home.

Do I need craft experience?

No. The shell is prepared in advance and our team guides you through the finishing, polishing and design stages.

What else can I see at the Impact Center?

Your visit also includes the Mr. Hero environmental experience, a plastic transformation demonstration and access to a collection of products sourced from local artisan and craftsman families.

Is herbal tea included?

Yes. Complimentary herbal tea is included with the experience.

Where is the experience?

The workshop takes place at the ZeroPlastic Impact Center near Sigiriya, Sri Lanka, roughly 5 km before Sigiriya Lion Rock.
