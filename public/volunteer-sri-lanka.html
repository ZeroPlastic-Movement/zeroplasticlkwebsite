Who you would be joining

A Sri Lankan movement, built and led by young people

ZeroPlastic Movement is Sri Lanka's largest youth-led environmental initiative and a government-registered non-profit organization established in 2021. The work happens through clubs, campaigns and community projects across the country.

International volunteers are not a separate programme running alongside that work. You join the teams already doing it, contributing where there is a genuine need and where your skills and available time actually fit.

Volunteer opportunities depend on current project needs, location, availability and suitability. That is why every application is reviewed individually rather than booked from a calendar.

Three pathways

Choose where your contribution fits

Each pathway leads to a different kind of placement. Tell us which interests you and we will match it against what is actually running while you are in Sri Lanka.

Pathway one

Volunteer at the Impact Center in Sigiriya

Support the day-to-day mission of the ZeroPlastic Impact Center, the movement's visitor-facing home near Sigiriya, where plastic alternatives and Sri Lankan craftsmanship are shown to travellers every day.

Where you might contribute

  • Supporting visitor experiences
  • Helping promote sustainable Sri Lankan products
  • Assisting with responsible tourism activities
  • Supporting environmental education
  • Helping document stories and impact
  • Supporting communications and digital content
  • Assisting with events and community engagement
  • Contributing professional skills where relevant

Suits: volunteers who enjoy working with people, are comfortable in a visitor-facing setting and can commit to a consistent weekly rhythm. Placements are matched to current operational needs and to your experience.

Pathway two

Support local artisans

ZeroPlastic works with 249 local artisan families. This pathway is a skills exchange: you bring capabilities their businesses can use, and you learn craft and enterprise from people who have practised it for years.

Where you might contribute

  • Product storytelling
  • Photography and content
  • Digital promotion
  • English communication
  • Packaging and presentation
  • Business skills
  • Market research
  • Responsible tourism experiences

Suits: volunteers with a practical skill a small enterprise can apply, and the patience to work at the pace of a working household. Placements are arranged only after suitability, consent, safeguarding and logistical review.

Pathway three

Join environmental action projects

Work on the environmental campaigns the movement is running while you are here, from coastal cleanups to university chapter programmes and the reporting that turns a day of work into evidence.

Where you might contribute

  • Plastic cleanups
  • Community awareness activities
  • Tree-planting projects
  • University chapter programmes
  • Plastic collection and environmental campaigns
  • Project documentation and impact reporting

Suits: volunteers who want to be on the ground and can travel to where a project is running. Specific activities, dates and locations depend on currently approved projects, so a cleanup or tree-planting day cannot be guaranteed for every date.

The programme base

Sigiriya, where the Impact Center sits

The ZeroPlastic Impact Center is located near Sigiriya, in Sri Lanka's cultural triangle. It is the base for Impact Center placements and the point where the movement's work meets the people travelling through the country.

Sigiriya itself needs little introduction: the rock fortress, the surrounding forest and the villages and farmland around it. Living and working here for a few weeks is a different experience from passing through for an afternoon.

Placements on the other two pathways may be based elsewhere, depending on which projects and artisan communities are active during your dates.

Programme fees

A structured, paid volunteer programme

ZeroPlastic volunteer placements carry a programme fee. Being straightforward about that from the start is part of how the programme is run.

Coordinating an international placement takes real work before you arrive and while you are here. The programme fee is what funds that work rather than diverting it from the projects themselves.

What the programme fee supports

  • Programme coordination across your placement, from application to departure.
  • Orientation when you arrive, so you can contribute from the first week.
  • Project matching against what is genuinely running and needed.
  • Administration including suitability review and safeguarding checks.
  • Local impact delivery, the on-the-ground capacity that makes hosting a volunteer possible.

Programme fees vary according to the selected project, duration and level of support required. You will receive the applicable programme fee and full cost breakdown before confirming your placement.

Accommodation, transportation, meals, flights, visas, insurance and other personal expenses are separate unless a specific programme package explicitly includes them. Where a package does include something, it will be itemised in the breakdown you receive.

What volunteers receive

What is included in every placement

  • Pre-arrival programme information covering your placement and what to prepare.
  • Placement matching against current project needs and your own skills.
  • Local orientation when you arrive in Sri Lanka.
  • ZeroPlastic programme coordination throughout your placement.
  • Project guidance from the team you are working alongside.
  • Participation acknowledgement or certificate, subject to verified participation.
  • Opportunities to learn about environmental and community challenges in Sri Lanka.

Responsibility and ethics

How we decide what a volunteer does

  • Placements are based on real project needs, not on creating activity for visitors.
  • Community and artisan consent is required before any placement involving them proceeds.
  • Volunteers respect local culture, privacy and safeguarding rules at all times.
  • Participation certificates require verified attendance.
  • Activities may change due to weather, permissions, safety or community requirements.

We do not place volunteers where a local person should be employed, and we do not arrange visits to communities as an attraction. If a placement would not genuinely help, we will tell you and suggest something that would.

Questions

Before you apply

Who can apply?

International travellers, gap-year volunteers, university students, professionals and responsible travellers are all welcome to apply. Placements depend on current project needs, location, availability and suitability, so every application is reviewed individually.

Do I need environmental experience?

No. Many placements need practical skills such as photography, communications, design, business support or general project help rather than environmental training. Tell us what you are good at and we will look for where it fits.

Is this a free volunteer programme?

No. This is a structured, paid volunteer programme and participants pay a programme fee. You will receive the applicable programme fee and full cost breakdown before confirming your placement.

What does the programme fee cover?

The programme fee supports programme coordination, orientation, project matching, administration and local impact delivery. The amount depends on the selected project, duration and level of support required.

Is accommodation included?

Accommodation, transportation, meals, flights, visas, insurance and other personal expenses are separate unless a specific programme package explicitly includes them. Anything that is included will be itemised in the cost breakdown you receive.

Can I volunteer with an artisan?

It may be possible. ZeroPlastic works with 249 local artisan families, and volunteers may have opportunities to work with selected artisans on areas such as product storytelling, photography, digital promotion and business skills. Placements are arranged only after suitability, consent, safeguarding and logistical review, and we do not promise that every volunteer will stay or work inside a private residence.

Can groups or universities apply?

Yes. Indicate the number of people travelling on the application form and describe the group in the contribution field. Group placements depend on project capacity during your dates.

Will a cleanup or tree-planting project always be available?

No. Specific activities, dates and locations depend on currently approved projects, so we cannot guarantee a cleanup or tree-planting activity on every date. Activities may also change due to weather, permissions, safety or community requirements.

Do I receive a certificate?

A participation acknowledgement or certificate is available subject to verified participation. Certificates require verified attendance.

Do I need a visa or travel insurance?

Visa, insurance and medical requirements depend on your nationality and your circumstances, and they change. ZeroPlastic does not provide legal, immigration or medical advice. Please verify the current requirements independently with the relevant Sri Lankan authorities, your own government and a qualified insurance or medical provider before you travel.

How are placements confirmed?

After you apply, ZeroPlastic reviews suitability and availability, then sends you the programme details and full cost breakdown. The placement is confirmed only after you have reviewed that information and accepted it.
